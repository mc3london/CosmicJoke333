About Us:

"Cosmic Joke 333" is a new organization releasing monthly writings and video essays about life, suffering, overcoming struggles, and exploring spirituality to raise money to feed all the kids at a single underfunded primary school in Bodh Gaya, India: THE SAKYA SUNJATA PRIMARY SCHOOL.

If you enjoy reading and watching, consider donating to our GoFundMe to support the Sakya Sujata Children Welfare Trust Primary School in Bodh Gaya, India.

This school's motto is "Compassion is the Root of Religion."

Links to Explore External Resources & Other Causes to Support:

  • Love Serve Remember Foundation (LSRF): The main nonprofit organization dedicated to preserving, protecting, and broadcasting the teachings of Ram Dass and his guru, Neem Karoli Baba, via the Be Here Now Network podcasts, digital archives, and online courses. [1]
  • Hanuman Foundation: Established by Ram Dass in 1974 to embody the spirit of service, famous for housing early seminal works (like publishing Be Here Now) and spawning conscious living and dying initiatives. [1, 2]
  • Seva Foundation: A global nonprofit eye care and international health organization co-founded by Ram Dass, Larry Brilliant, and others in 1978 to reverse avoidable blindness and alleviate suffering. [1, 2]
  • Living / Dying Project: The Living/Dying Project offers conscious and compassionate support in the spirit of mutual exploration to people facing life-threatening illness, to their caregivers, to folks facing life's most difficult situations, and to anyone committed to spiritual transformation.
  • Together We Bake: Supporting women with low income who are navigating barriers to employment and are motivated to transform their lives. Through our innovative nonprofit social enterprise, they prepare program participants for quality jobs that offer not just employment but pathways to opportunity, growth, and family-sustaining wages. Their goal is that each woman who walks through our doors leaves with the tools and support needed to create a better future for herself, her family, and her community.
  • Wright's Law: For more than 25 years, parents, educators, advocates, and attorneys have relied on WrightsLaw for accurate, practical information about special education law and advocacy. WrightsLaw is a leading online resource providing accurate, practical information about special education law, education-related case law, and advocacy strategies for children with disabilities.
  • MAPS: The Multidisciplinary Association of Psychedelic Studiers (MAPS) is a nonprofit organization that provides public resources and leadership as we work together to create legal responsible evidence–based pathways to psychedelics. Since 1986, MAPS has worked tirelessly to build and share the blueprint for ending psychedelic prohibition. This MAPS blueprint is being used throughout the world to take an evidence-based approach to psychedelic healing. As a nonprofit, MAPS has relied on the support of people like you to pursue our mission, build a movement, and change the way people think about, talk about, and consume psychedelics through research, education, and advocacy.
