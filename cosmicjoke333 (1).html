Links to Explore External Resources & Other Causes to Support:

Love Serve Remember Foundation (LSRF):

The main nonprofit organization dedicated to preserving, protecting, and broadcasting the teachings of Ram Dass and his guru, Neem Karoli Baba, via the Be Here Now Network podcasts, digital archives, and online courses.

Hanuman Foundation:

Established by Ram Dass in 1974 to embody the spirit of service, famous for housing early seminal works (like publishing Be Here Now) and spawning conscious living and dying initiatives.

Seva Foundation:

A global nonprofit eye care and international health organization co-founded by Ram Dass, Larry Brilliant, and others in 1978 to reverse avoidable blindness and alleviate suffering.

Living / Dying Project:

Offers conscious and compassionate support in the spirit of mutual exploration to people facing life-threatening illness, their caregivers, and anyone committed to spiritual transformation.

Together We Bake:

Supporting women with low income who are navigating barriers to employment, preparing program participants for quality jobs with pathways to opportunity, growth, and family-sustaining wages.

Wright's Law:

For more than 25 years, parents, educators, advocates, and attorneys have relied on WrightsLaw for accurate, practical information about special education law and advocacy.

MAPS:

The Multidisciplinary Association of Psychedelic Studies is a nonprofit that provides public resources and leadership to create legal, responsible, evidence-based pathways to psychedelics, since 1986.
